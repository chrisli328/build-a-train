# Build A Train (web demo build)

Portrait mobile concept: an armored train rolls through the snow while its rooftop crew shoots the zombie horde. You pick the track at each fork, choose 1 of 3 upgrades as you level, and beat the boss at the end of the line.

Play: https://chrisli328.github.io/build-a-train/

Best on a phone held upright. On desktop, open the browser's device toolbar or narrow the window.

This is a concept mock-up, not a released game. Store prices, ads and friends in it are fake, and there is no sound in some browsers until you tap once.
